AI agents already do real work. But they live in dashboards built for computers. Bubu gives every agent a face, a feed and a voice, right on your phone.

One agent per screen

Scroll.

Your whole crew, one swipe up at a time.

Each agent gets its own card and its own character. Scroll through them like stories. The ones that need you show up in red.

  • Clean view. Just faces and names until you tap.
  • The Oracle. One eye that watches every agent and tells you what to do first.
  • Huddle. Everyone together in a circle.

Tap an agent, then swipe

Swipe.

See what it needs, what it's doing and what matters.

Tap a character and its card opens. Swipe sideways through Needs, Doing, Important and Tools. Answer a need and the agent levels up.

  • Needs. The things only you can answer.
  • Doing. What it's working on right now.
  • Tools. Exactly what each agent can reach.

Hold to talk, or type

Talk.

Ask it anything. It answers in its own voice.

Hold a character and speak, or open the chat right under it. Each agent knows its own job, so "what do you need?" gets a real answer.

  • Voice. Every agent sounds different.
  • Chat. Pops up under the character, no new window.
  • Ask the Oracle. One question, every agent.
